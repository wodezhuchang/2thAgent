# Earthquakes 教学设计

实习生：卢立春（北京师范大学外国语言文学学院 英语师范）；指导教师：陈则航、李芸；实习学校：贵阳市第八中学；班级：高一（1）（2）班。

## Teaching Objectives
By the end of this class, students will be able to:
1. Conclude the signs, process, damages and rescue works of the Tangshan Earthquake.
2. Recognize the form, meaning and pragmatic functions of key vocabulary (pipe, extreme, at the end, burst, dam, useless, in ruins, be trapped etc.).
3. Act as a survivor and retell the process, damages and rescue according to key words.
4. Realize the author's emotional attitude and reflect on natural disasters and social warmth.

## Key / Difficult Points
Key: conclude main idea of each paragraph; obtain form/meaning/pragmatic function of new words in context; sense author's emotion and conduct critical text interaction.
Difficult: emotional attitude & retelling as a survivor.

## Duration & Method
1 period; lecture + discussion. Aids: textbook, blackboard, chalk, multimedia.

## Teaching Procedure
Pre-reading
1. Show pictures about earthquakes, ask what happened (arouse interest).
2. Pair work: what causes earthquakes? Any omens? (activate prior knowledge).
3. Name famous earthquakes, elicit Tangshan Earthquake.
4. Pair work: discuss the title and predict content.
While-reading
5. First reading: skim and conclude main idea of each paragraph.
6. Second reading: verbal/reorganized questions + gap-filling, paragraph by paragraph; explain important words in use.
7. Third reading: comprehension questions for discussion (sense emotional mood, think about natural disasters).
Post-reading
8. Pair interview: one as reporter, the other as a survivor.
9. Homework: using given key words, retell the process and damages of Tangshan Earthquake and write the story.

## Blackboard Design
Unit 4 Earthquakes: omens / process / damages / rescue work → "A night the earth didn't sleep"; mood: serious, sad, hope. Key phrases: too...to..., at an end, right away, dig out, a (great) number of, give out, thousands of, in ruins.
